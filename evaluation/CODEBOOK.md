# Codebook: analogies with the past

Read the **TARGET** sentence with its context. The crisis and its start date are given above it.

## 1. Analogy? (yes / no)

**yes**: the TARGET sentence itself sets the present beside a past that lies **before the crisis began** and is
**separate from it**. The present is the crisis, or the situation the sentence describes. The past may be shown as
- alike or a repetition ("weer een kaping", "net als in 1953", "doet denken aan Wijster"),
- unchanged since ("zoals altijd sinds de oorlog"),
- different or worse ("erger dan de watersnood van 1916"),
- unprecedented ("nog nooit eerder", "voor het eerst sinds 1961").

Implicit comparisons count.

**no** when
- there is no past at all;
- a past is only *mentioned* (as cause, date, biography or history) without being set beside the present;
- the "past" is the crisis's own earlier course ("gisteren", "de eerste dag van de kaping");
- the sentence looks ahead ("dit kan zich herhalen");
- the yardstick is what is normal or usual ("drukker dan normaal", "zoals gebruikelijk");
- the comparison is with another place now ("ook in België");
- the comparison is made only in a neighbouring sentence, not in the TARGET.

If you hesitate, choose what fits best and tick **unsure**. Unsure items are reviewed together.

## 2. Only when analogy = yes

**direction**
- *similarity*: alike, again, unchanged since.
- *rupture*: different, worse, better, never before, first since.

**kind**
- *named_event*: one specific event or date ("treinkaping Wijster", "1953").
- *series*: several earlier cases ("eerdere gijzelingen", "vorige rampen").
- *whole_past*: all of history ("ooit", "nog nooit", "alle eerdere rampen").
- *general_period*: a bounded era or the situation before ("vroeger", "de jaren dertig", "voor de oorlog").

**past referred to**: a few words, e.g. "treinkaping Wijster, 1975" or "eerdere overstromingen".

## Rules of thumb
- Judge only the TARGET sentence; the context is there to understand it.
- OCR errors: read through them; do not mark no because of bad OCR.
- "weer" or "opnieuw" about the crisis itself (a second explosion the same day) is **no**.
- "voor het eerst" without a past ("voor het eerst sprak de minister") is **no**; "voor het eerst sinds 1961" is **yes**.
